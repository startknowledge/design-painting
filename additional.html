Project Background

Design Painting was conceived as a visual celebration of India's rich handicraft painting traditions. It is part of the larger StartKnowledge ecosystem, which aims to create accessible, educational, and inspiring digital experiences. This gallery focuses on the formal elements – line, color, light, space, composition, and style – to help viewers appreciate the technical and aesthetic mastery behind each artwork.

The project is built with a modern web stack (HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Three.js) and is designed to be fully responsive, ensuring a seamless experience across all devices.

Acknowledgements

We extend our deepest gratitude to the artisans, craftsmen, and tribal communities who have preserved and enriched these painting traditions for centuries. Their skill, creativity, and cultural wisdom are the true heart of this gallery. We also thank the scholars, researchers, and enthusiasts who continue to study and document these art forms.

We believe that art connects us to our roots and inspires our future. This gallery is a humble tribute to that enduring legacy.
