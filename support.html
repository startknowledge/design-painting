Report a Technical Issue

If you encounter a problem – such as images not loading, navigation errors, or display issues – please provide us with as much detail as possible:

  • Browser and version (e.g., Chrome 116).
  • Device type (desktop, tablet, mobile).
  • Steps to reproduce – what you did before the issue occurred.
  • Expected result vs. actual result.
  • Screenshots (if helpful).

Send your report to support@startknowledge.in. We typically respond within 24‑48 hours.

Artist & Artwork Inquiries

If you have questions about a specific artwork, style, or would like to connect with artisans, please email us at contact@startknowledge.in. We will do our best to provide accurate background information or put you in touch with relevant sources.

Privacy & Data Concerns

We take your privacy seriously. If you have concerns about how your data is handled, please review our Privacy Policy and Cookies Policy. For specific inquiries, contact contact@startknowledge.in.

Response Times

  • General inquiries: 48‑72 hours.
  • Technical issues: 24‑48 hours for initial acknowledgment.
  • Urgent matters: We will strive to respond sooner.

Response times may be longer during weekends, holidays, or high‑volume periods.
