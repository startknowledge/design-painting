Response Times

We aim to respond to all inquiries in a timely manner. The table below provides an indication of expected response times based on the nature of your inquiry. Please note that during holidays, weekends, or high-volume periods, response times may be longer. We appreciate your patience and understanding.

  • General Questions & Feedback: Usually within 48–72 hours.
  • Artist & Artwork Inquiries: Usually within 72 hours; we may need to consult with our experts or sources.
  • Technical Issues & Bug Reports: Usually within 24–48 hours for initial acknowledgment.
  • Feature Requests & Suggestions: Usually within 72 hours; we may follow up for clarification.
  • Partnership & Collaboration: Usually within 3–5 working days.

We value every message and strive to provide meaningful responses that address your concerns or curiosity about our collection.

Before You Contact Us

To help us assist you more efficiently, please consider the following suggestions before reaching out. Many common questions and topics are already covered in our existing pages.

  • Check the FAQ: Many common questions are already answered in the Frequently Asked Questions page – including details about painting styles, techniques, and usage.
  • Review Documentation: The Documentation page contains technical information about painting materials, processes, and regional variations.
  • Read the About Page: The About page provides a deep dive into the formal elements (line, color, light, space, composition) and historical context of the artworks.
  • Check the Disclaimer: The Disclaimer explains the limitations, responsibilities, and intended use of the gallery.
  • Review the Changelog: The Changelog shows recent updates, fixes, and improvements made to the project.

If you have already checked these resources and still need assistance, please use the contact form or email addresses above. Be sure to include relevant details such as the artwork you are inquiring about, your browser (for technical issues), and any specific questions you have.

Privacy & Data Handling

Your privacy is important to us. When you contact us through the contact form or via email, we use the information you provide solely for the purpose of responding to your inquiry. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

For more details about how we handle information, please read our Privacy Policy and Cookies Policy.

Secure Communication: While we take reasonable measures to protect the information you send to us, no email or web form is completely secure. Please avoid sending sensitive, confidential, or classified information through these channels.
