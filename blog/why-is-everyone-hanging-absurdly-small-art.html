  • Make it personal: Choose small art pieces that reflect your **personal style** and **interests**. This will help create a **sense of connection** to the artwork and make it more enjoyable to live with.

Common Mistakes to Avoid When Hanging Small Art

While small art can be a great addition to any space, there are some common mistakes to avoid when hanging it. Here are a few things to keep in mind:

  • Avoid overcrowding: Too many small art pieces can create a **cluttered** and **overwhelming** look. Make sure to leave some **breathing room** between each piece.
  • Consider the scale: Make sure the small art piece is in proportion to the **furniture** and **decor** in the room. A small piece can get lost in a large room, so consider using multiple pieces or a larger frame to create a **balanced** look.
  • Don't forget about lighting: Small art pieces can be **sensitive to light**, so make sure to consider the lighting in the room when placing them. **Harsh** or **direct** light can damage the artwork, so consider using **UV-filtering glass** or **shades** to protect it.
