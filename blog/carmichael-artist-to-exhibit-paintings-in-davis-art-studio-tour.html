Common Mistakes to Avoid at Art Studio Tours

Even the most enthusiastic art lovers can make unintentional mistakes that detract from the experience. Here’s what to avoid at the Davis Art Studio Tour:

1. Skipping the Artist Bios

Mistake: Assuming all artists are equally skilled without researching. Solution: Read bios beforehand—some artists may have unique techniques or backstories that enrich your experience.

2. Being Too Hesitant to Engage

Mistake: Standing silently in front of a painting without asking questions. Solution: Approach artists with curiosity—most welcome conversation.

3. Focusing Only on Price, Not Artistry

Mistake: Dismissing a piece because it’s "too expensive" without considering its value and craftsmanship. Solution: Compare works to your budget—sometimes, a smaller piece is a better investment than a large, overpriced one.

4. Not Following Up After the Tour

Mistake: Losing track of artists you loved after the event. Solution: Save their contact info and follow up via email or social media.

5. Ignoring the "Hidden" Artists

Mistake: Only visiting the most famous studios. Solution: Explore every space—you might discover a future star.

6. Not Asking About Shipping or Logistics

Mistake: Assuming you can take a piece home immediately. Solution: Clarify shipping policies before purchasing—some artists offer free local delivery.

7. Being Too Impatient

Mistake: Expecting to buy a major piece on the first visit. Solution: Take your time—some of the best connections happen over multiple visits.
