Common Mistakes to Avoid When Exhibiting Your Art

Even the most talented artists can sabotage their own success with avoidable mistakes. Here’s what to watch out for:

1. Overlooking the Importance of Branding

Many artists don’t treat their exhibition like a business. Without a strong brand identity (logo, consistent color scheme, professional website), your work can blend in with others.

Solution:

  • Develop a cohesive visual identity (use Canva or hire a designer).
  • Ensure your website and social media are updated with exhibition details.

2. Not Setting Clear Goals

Without specific objectives, it’s hard to measure success. Ask yourself:

  • Do I want to sell pieces?
  • Do I want to network with galleries?
  • Do I want to build my social media following?

Example: An artist who only focused on sales missed out on a gallery offer because they didn’t engage with curators.

3. Underestimating the Power of Networking

Some artists hide in their studios and avoid interacting with visitors. But networking is how careers grow.

Solution:

  • Schedule "artist meet-and-greets" during the event.
  • Exchange contact info with other exhibitors for future collaborations.

4. Not Preparing for Logistics

Last-minute shipping, damaged frames, or technical issues can ruin an exhibition. Always:

  • Insure your artwork.
  • Test lighting and display setups beforehand.
  • Have backup pieces in case of accidents.

5. Ignoring Post-Exhibition Follow-Up

Many artists disappear after the event, losing potential buyers and connections. Follow up within 48 hours with:

  • A thank-you email.
  • A discount code for future purchases.
  • An invitation to a private viewing of new work.

6. Overpricing or Underpricing Your Work

  • Overpricing can scare away buyers.
  • Underpricing undervalues your talent.

Solution:

  • Research similar artists’ pricing in your area.
  • Consider payment plans for high-end pieces.

7. Not Leveraging Local Media

Many artists don’t pitch their story to newspapers or blogs. A single feature can boost sales exponentially.

Solution:

  • Write a press release highlighting your exhibition.
  • Offer exclusive interviews to local outlets.
