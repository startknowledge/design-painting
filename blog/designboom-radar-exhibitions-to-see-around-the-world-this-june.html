Must-See Exhibitions this June

This June, the designboom radar highlights a range of captivating exhibitions that span across the globe, from major museums to independent galleries. Here are 8-10 actionable strategies or tips to help you make the most of your exhibition experience:

  • **Plan ahead**: Research the exhibitions and create a schedule to ensure you don't miss any of the must-see shows.
  • **Arrive early**: Get to the exhibition venue early to avoid crowds and have a more intimate experience with the artworks.
  • **Take notes**: Bring a notebook and jot down your thoughts, impressions, and observations about the artworks and exhibitions.
  • **Engage with the artists**: Attend artist talks, workshops, and panel discussions to gain deeper insights into the creative process and inspiration behind the works.
  • **Explore the venue**: Take time to appreciate the architecture and design of the exhibition space, which can often be a work of art in itself.
  • **Network and connect**: Attend exhibition openings and events to meet fellow art enthusiasts, collectors, and industry professionals.
  • **Stay curious**: Be open to new and unconventional art forms, and don't be afraid to ask questions or seek guidance from exhibition staff.
  • **Support emerging artists**: Look out for exhibitions featuring emerging artists and consider purchasing their works to support their careers.
  • **Document your experience**: Share your thoughts, photos, and reviews on social media to inspire others and create a community around the exhibitions.
  • **Follow up**: After the exhibition, follow up with the artists, galleries, or museums to stay updated on future events and exhibitions.
