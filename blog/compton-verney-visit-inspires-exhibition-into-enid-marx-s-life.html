Introduction to Enid Marx

Enid Marx was a British designer, artist, and educator, born in 1902 in London. She is best known for her work in textile design, particularly her bold and colorful patterns for fabrics, wallpapers, and ceramics. Throughout her career, Marx was influenced by various art movements, including **Art Deco** and **Bauhaus**, which is reflected in her unique and innovative designs. The Compton Verney exhibition showcases a wide range of Marx's work, including her early designs for the **London Underground** and her later work as a **Royal Designer for Industry**.
