Introduction to Oxcart Painting

Oxcart painting is a traditional Costa Rican art form that dates back to the early 20th century. The vibrant and colorful designs, which often feature floral patterns, geometric shapes, and animal motifs, are painted on wooden oxcarts, which were originally used for transportation and farming purposes. Today, these carts have become an integral part of Costa Rican culture and identity, with many artists and artisans continuing to create and innovate within this traditional art form.
