Strategies for Creating an Inclusive Design Education Environment

So, what can institutions of higher learning do to create a more inclusive environment for students of all genders? Here are 8-10 actionable strategies or tips:

  • **Offer resources and support services** specifically for non-binary and gender-nonconforming students, such as counseling and academic advising.
  • **Foster a sense of community** among students, faculty, and staff, through events and activities that promote inclusivity and respect for all individuals.
  • **Incorporate diverse and inclusive curricula** that reflect the experiences and perspectives of students of all backgrounds, including non-binary and gender-nonconforming students.
  • **Encourage student feedback and participation** in decision-making processes, to ensure that the needs and concerns of all students are heard and addressed.
  • **Develop partnerships with local organizations** that serve non-binary and gender-nonconforming communities, to provide students with opportunities for internships, mentorship, and career development.
  • **Celebrate and promote diversity and inclusivity** through events and activities, such as art exhibitions, performances, and lectures that showcase the work and perspectives of non-binary and gender-nonconforming artists and designers.
  • **Continuously assess and evaluate** the effectiveness of inclusive policies and practices, to ensure that the institution is meeting the needs of all students and creating a welcoming and supportive environment.
