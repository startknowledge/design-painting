Common Mistakes and How to Avoid Them

While incorporating artistic inspirations into your design painting can be a powerful way to add depth and emotion, there are also common mistakes to avoid. One of the most significant pitfalls is copying rather than interpreting. Rather than simply reproducing the style of another artist, take the time to understand the underlying principles and techniques that make their work unique, and use those as a starting point for your own creative exploration. Another mistake is failing to edit and refine. Don't be afraid to take risks and try new things, but also be willing to step back and evaluate your work, making adjustments and edits as needed to create a cohesive and polished piece.
