8–10 Actionable Strategies for Bringing New Dimensions to Painting

So, how can artists and designers bring new dimensions to their work, just like Charles Hinman did? Here are 10 actionable strategies to get you started:

  • Use technology to enhance your artwork: Technology can be a powerful tool for artists and designers. Consider using digital tools, such as computer-aided design (CAD) software or 3D printing, to create complex shapes and forms.
  • Take risks and be open to failure: Creating innovative and groundbreaking artwork often requires taking risks and being open to failure. Don't be afraid to try new things and experiment with different techniques and materials.
  • Draw inspiration from the world around you: The world around us is full of inspiration and ideas. Draw inspiration from nature, architecture, and other sources to create a unique and innovative artwork.
  • Stay true to your vision and artistic voice: Above all, stay true to your vision and artistic voice. Don't be swayed by trends or what others think you should be doing. Create artwork that is authentic and meaningful to you.
