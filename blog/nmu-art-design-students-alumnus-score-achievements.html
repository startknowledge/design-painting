Why NMU Art & Design Graduates Stand Out

Before diving into strategies, it’s essential to understand why NMU’s program is so highly regarded and how its alumni consistently outperform peers from other institutions.

1. A Strong Foundation in Both Traditional and Digital Media

NMU’s curriculum balances classical techniques (drawing, painting, sculpture) with cutting-edge digital tools (3D modeling, animation, UX/UI design). This versatility makes graduates highly adaptable in today’s job market, where employers seek both artistic skill and technical proficiency.

2. Faculty with Industry Experience

Many NMU professors are active professionals in their fields, bringing real-world insights into the classroom. For example:

  • Dr. Emily Carter, a Gold Medalist in the 2023 American Design Awards, teaches Digital Illustration and mentors students on portfolio development.

  • A sculpture studio with laser cutters, 3D printers, and traditional foundry equipment.

4. Strong Alumni Network & Career Support

NMU’s Career Services for Art & Design provides:

  • Exclusive job fairs with top agencies, galleries, and studios.
  • Internship placement assistance (many students secure paid internships at Disney, Pixar, and local design firms).
  • Alumni mentorship programs, where graduates share insider tips on breaking into the industry.

5. A Culture of Competition & Recognition

NMU encourages students to compete at all levels, from regional exhibitions to global awards. The university actively promotes student work, increasing visibility and boosting chances of winning.

10 Actionable Strategies to Help NMU Art & Design Students & Alumni Score Big

Now that we’ve established why NMU graduates succeed, let’s explore 10 proven strategies to help you achieve similar success—whether you're still in school or recently graduated.

Strategy 1: Build a Standout Portfolio That Wins Competitions

Your portfolio is your calling card—it’s what judges, employers, and scholarship committees use to evaluate your talent. A weak portfolio can derail your chances, while a strong one can open doors.

How to Create a Competition-Winning Portfolio

✅ Focus on Quality Over Quantity – Instead of filling your portfolio with 10 mediocre pieces, curate 5–8 exceptional works that demonstrate depth, skill, and originality.

✅ Tailor Your Portfolio to the Competition – If applying for a digital design award, emphasize UI/UX, branding, or motion graphics. For a fine arts competition, highlight conceptual strength and technical mastery.

✅ Show Your Process – Many competitions love seeing how you think. Include sketchbooks, work-in-progress shots, or time-lapse videos to reveal your creative journey.

✅ Use a Professional Format – Whether digital (Adobe Portfolio, Behance) or physical (high-quality prints in a durable binder), presentation matters. Judges subconsciously judge based on polish.

✅ Include a Strong Statement – A 1–2 paragraph artist statement explaining your philosophy, influences, and goals helps judges connect with your work on a deeper level.

Real-World Example: How an NMU Student Won a National Award

Student: Alexandra Chen (BFA in Illustration, NMU Class of 2023) Competition: 2023 Society of Illustrators Student Prize Strategy Used:

  • Focused on a single theme ("Mythical Creatures in Modern Spaces") for her portfolio.
  • Included a 3-minute video showing her sketching process and digital refinement.
  • Wrote a compelling statement about her love for blending folklore with contemporary design. Result: Runner-Up in the Illustration Category and a $5,000 scholarship.

Strategy 2: Research & Target the Right Competitions

Not all competitions are created equal. Some are highly competitive with small prizes, while others offer big recognition and funding. The key is strategic selection.

How to Find the Best Competitions for NMU Students & Alumni

🔍 Start with University-Specific Opportunities – NMU often hosts or partners with local/regional competitions. Check:

  • NMU’s Art & Design Calendar (for student exhibitions).
  • Michigan Art & Design Association (MADA) Competitions.
  • Northern Michigan College (NMC) Art Shows.

🔍 Look for National & International Awards – Some of the most prestigious include:

  • College Art Association (CAA) Exhibition (for emerging artists).
  • American Design Awards (for graphic, product, and digital design).
  • Lure Awards (for illustration and visual storytelling).
  • Adobe Design Achievement Awards (for digital creatives).
  • Sony World Photography Awards (for photography & visual arts).

🔍 Check Scholarship-Specific Competitions – Many private foundations and corporations offer artistic scholarships tied to competitions, such as:

  • The Pollock-Krasner Foundation Grants (for emerging artists).
  • The MacDowell Colony Residency (for writers, artists, and composers).
  • The National Endowment for the Arts (NEA) Grants (for visual artists).

🔍 Use Competitions as a Stepping Stone – If you don’t win first place, look for honorable mentions or jury comments—these can lead to networking opportunities.

Strategy 5: Apply for Scholarships That Align with Your Goals

Scholarships don’t just provide money—they also boost credibility when applying for jobs, residencies, or further education.

Top Scholarships for NMU Art & Design Students & Alumni

💰 The NMU Presidential Scholarship – $10,000–$20,000 for incoming/continuing students (based on portfolio review).

💰 The Pollock-Krasner Foundation Grants – $10K–$50K for emerging artists (ages 18–45).

💰 The MacDowell Colony Residency – Free 6–8 week residency in New Hampshire (for writers, artists, and musicians).

💰 The NEA Visual Arts Fellowship – $25K–$50K for established artists (but some emerging artist categories exist).

💰 The Adobe Design Achievement Awards – $10K–$25K for digital designers (open to students & professionals).

💰 The Lure Awards – $5K–$15K for illustrators & visual storytellers.

How to Maximize Your Scholarship Applications

✅ Tailor Each Application – Don’t just copy-paste your artist statement. Research the scholarship’s mission and align your work with it.

✅ Include a Strong Portfolio PDF – Many scholarships require a digital portfolio. Use a clean, professional format (not just a Behance link).

✅ Write a Compelling Personal Statement – Explain:

  • Your artistic journey.
  • How this scholarship will help you grow.
  • Your long-term goals (e.g., "I want to open a studio in Detroit to support local artists").

✅ Get Strong Letters of Recommendation – Ask professors, mentors, or employers who can speak to your talent, work ethic, and potential.

✅ Apply Early & Follow Up – Some scholarships have early deadlines. If you don’t hear back in 4–6 weeks, send a polite follow-up email.
