The Role of Art in Shaping Careers: Lessons from SIU Carbondale Twins

Art is more than a hobby; it’s a versatile skill set that translates into nearly every industry. For Alex and Jordan, their shared love of art wasn’t just about filling sketchbooks—it was about developing critical thinking, adaptability, and visual storytelling, all of which are highly valued in design careers. According to a 2023 survey by Creative Bloq, **78% of design professionals** credit their artistic training for their ability to innovate and solve complex problems.

At SIU Carbondale, twins like Alex and Jordan have access to a robust arts program that includes:

  • Diverse mediums: From traditional painting and sculpture to digital design and fashion illustration.
  • Faculty mentorship: Professors who guide students in refining their techniques and building professional portfolios.
  • Collaborative spaces: Shared studios and workshops that encourage experimentation and peer feedback.
  • Industry connections: Guest lectures, internships, and alumni networks that open doors to real-world opportunities.

For twins, the advantage is twofold: they can learn from each other’s strengths while also developing their own unique voices. This duality is something we’ll explore further in the strategies section.

Why Twins Have an Edge in the Creative Industry

Being twins often means sharing a deep understanding of each other’s creative process. This can lead to:

  • Natural collaboration: Twins can brainstorm ideas together, challenge each other’s perspectives, and create projects that feel both cohesive and distinct.
  • Shared resources: From art supplies to studio space, twins can optimize costs and efficiency.
  • A unique brand story: Their twin dynamic can become part of their personal brand, making them memorable in a competitive field.

However, this edge comes with challenges—such as avoiding creative burnout or blending individual identities. We’ll address these in the Common Mistakes section later.

8 Actionable Strategies to Turn Art Into a Career (Inspired by SIU Carbondale Twins)

Alex and Jordan didn’t wake up one day with fully realized careers—they strategically built their paths step by step. Below are eight proven strategies they’ve used (and continue to use) to turn their artistic passions into professional opportunities.

1. Build a Portfolio That Reflects Individuality (Even as Twins)

One of the biggest challenges twins face is standing out while staying connected. Alex and Jordan solved this by:

  • Creating distinct sub-brands: Alex focuses on digital illustration and branding, while Jordan specializes in textile design and surface pattern creation.
  • Using a shared website with separate galleries: Their site features a main page with their collaborative work and individual sections for each of their portfolios.
  • Highlighting their twin dynamic in their storytelling: In their artist statements, they mention how their shared upbringing influences their creative process without overshadowing their individual styles.

Actionable Tip: Use tools like Adobe Portfolio or Squarespace to create a cohesive online presence with clear sections for each twin’s work.

2. Experiment with Diverse Mediums to Find Your Niche

Twins often have the luxury of trying multiple mediums simultaneously. Alex and Jordan did this by:

  • Starting with traditional painting (oil, acrylic) to build foundational skills.
  • Transitioning to digital design (Procreate, Illustrator) for branding and illustration work.
  • Exploring textile and surface pattern design through software like Adobe Illustrator and Procreate.

Actionable Tip: Dedicate one project per month to a new medium. Track your progress in a journal to identify which areas excite you most.

3. Leverage University Resources (Beyond Just Classes)

SIU Carbondale offers more than just art classes—it provides:

  • Design studios with shared equipment: Twins can split costs on high-end tools like Wacom tablets or Photoshop licenses.
  • Internship and job placement services: Their career center connects students with local businesses and design firms.
  • Guest lectures and workshops: They’ve attended sessions led by industry professionals, giving them insider knowledge on trends.

Actionable Tip: Schedule a meeting with your university’s career services office to explore internship opportunities that align with your twin’s individual goals.

4. Collaborate Strategically (Without Blurring Your Brands)

Twins can create powerful collaborative work—if done intentionally. Alex and Jordan’s approach:

  • Define clear roles: One handles concept development, the other executes the design.
  • Create "twin-themed" projects: They’ve worked on a series called "Mirrored Stories", where each piece is a dual interpretation of a single theme.
  • Avoid over-reliance on twin projects: They ensure at least 70% of their portfolio is individual work.

Actionable Tip: Use project management tools like Trello or Asana to divide tasks and track progress.

5. Network Like a Pro (Even as Students)

Networking isn’t just for professionals—it’s a career accelerator for students too. Alex and Jordan:

  • Attended local art fairs and gallery openings to meet potential clients and collaborators.
  • Joined online communities like Behance and Instagram to connect with designers worldwide.
  • Reached out to alumni from SIU Carbondale for informational interviews.

Actionable Tip: Set a goal to attend one networking event per month. Follow up with new contacts via email or LinkedIn within 48 hours.

6. Develop a Side Hustle to Gain Experience

While waiting for full-time opportunities, Alex and Jordan launched a side hustle:

  • Custom illustration commissions: They offered personalized illustrations for clients like local cafes and small businesses.
