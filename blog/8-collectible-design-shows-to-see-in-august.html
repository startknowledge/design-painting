Actionable Strategies for Attending Collectible Design Shows

  • Plan your visit: Create a schedule for your visit, including the dates, times, and locations of the shows. Be sure to leave some flexibility in your schedule to account for any unexpected discoveries.
  • Network with designers and collectors: Collectible design shows provide an excellent opportunity to connect with designers, artists, and collectors. Be sure to introduce yourself, ask questions, and learn about their experiences and insights.

  • Attend lectures and workshops: Many collectible design shows offer lectures, workshops, and panels. Attend these events to learn more about the design process, industry trends, and best practices.
  • Join design communities: Joining design communities, such as online forums or social media groups, can help you connect with other design enthusiasts and stay informed about upcoming shows and events.
  • Set a budget: When attending collectible design shows, it's essential to set a budget and stick to it. This will help you avoid overspending and ensure you make thoughtful, informed purchasing decisions.
  • Consider the provenance: When purchasing a collectible design piece, consider the provenance, including the designer's reputation, the piece's history, and any relevant documentation.
  • Work with a reputable gallery or dealer: When purchasing a collectible design piece, work with a reputable gallery or dealer. This will help ensure you're buying an authentic piece and receiving a fair price.
  • Stay informed about industry trends: Stay informed about industry trends, including changes in the market, new designers, and emerging styles. This will help you make informed purchasing decisions and stay ahead of the curve.
