8 Actionable Strategies for Creating Bluish Green Walls

Here are 8 actionable strategies for creating bluish green walls, as shared by Olivia Wilde:

  • Start with a neutral base: Before applying any paint, make sure to prime your walls with a neutral base coat. This will help to create a smooth and even surface for your paint to adhere to.
  • Choose the right shade of blue: Bluish green walls can range in color from soft and pale to deep and rich. Choose a shade of blue that complements your existing decor and furniture.
  • Add a touch of green: To create a bluish green effect, add a touch of green to your paint color. You can do this by mixing a small amount of green paint with your blue paint.
  • Consider the 60-30-10 rule: When it comes to paint color, the 60-30-10 rule is a great guideline to follow. This rule states that 60% of your room should be a dominant color, 30% a secondary color, and 10% an accent color.
  • Use a high-quality paint: The type of paint you use can make a big difference in the overall look and feel of your walls. Choose a high-quality paint that is durable and long-lasting.
  • Apply multiple coats: To achieve a deep and rich color, apply multiple coats of paint to your walls. Make sure to allow each coat to dry completely before applying the next one.
  • Don't forget to test the color: Before committing to a specific paint color, make sure to test it out on a small area of your wall. This will give you an idea of how the color will look in different lighting conditions.
  • Consider the color of your trim: The color of your trim can greatly affect the overall look of your bluish green walls. Choose a trim color that complements your paint color and adds a touch of contrast to your room.

Common Mistakes to Avoid When Creating Bluish Green Walls

While bluish green walls can be a beautiful and unique addition to any room, there are several common mistakes to avoid when creating this look. One of the most common mistakes is using too much green in the paint color, which can result in a walls that look more green than blue. Another common mistake is not testing the paint color before applying it to the entire wall, which can result in a color that looks different than expected.

To avoid these mistakes, make sure to test the paint color on a small area of the wall before applying it to the entire surface. Also, start with a small amount of green paint and add more as needed, rather than adding too much green at once.

Frequently Asked Questions

Here are some frequently asked questions about creating bluish green walls, along with their answers:

What is the best paint color for bluish green walls?
The best paint color for bluish green walls will depend on your personal preferences and the specific look you are trying to achieve. However, some popular paint colors for bluish green walls include Sherwin-Williams' "Sea Salt" and Valspar's "Driftwood Grey".
How do I choose the right shade of blue for my bluish green walls?
To choose the right shade of blue for your bluish green walls, consider the color of your furniture and decor, as well as the natural lighting in the room. You can also test out different shades of blue on a small area of the wall to see which one looks best.
Can I use bluish green walls in a small room?
Yes, bluish green walls can be used in a small room, but it's best to use a lighter shade of blue to make the room feel larger. You can also pair the bluish green walls with light-colored furniture and accents to create a sense of openness and airiness.
How do I add a touch of green to my bluish green walls?
To add a touch of green to your bluish green walls, you can mix a small amount of green paint with your blue paint. Start with a small amount of green and add more as needed, until you achieve the desired color.
Can I use bluish green walls in a bedroom?
Yes, bluish green walls can be used in a bedroom, and can create a calming and relaxing atmosphere. Pair the bluish green walls with soft, creamy bedding and accents of natural wood to create a warm and inviting space.
