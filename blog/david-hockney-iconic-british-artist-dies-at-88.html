Introduction to David Hockney's Life and Work

David Hockney was born on July 9, 1937, in Bradford, England. He began his artistic journey at the Bradford College of Art, where he studied from 1953 to 1957. Hockney's early work was influenced by the **Pop Art** movement, which emerged in the 1950s. His unique style, which blended elements of **realism** and **abstraction**, quickly gained recognition, and he became one of the most prominent figures in the British art scene. Throughout his career, Hockney experimented with various mediums, including painting, printmaking, photography, and even **stage design**.
