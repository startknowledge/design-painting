Whether you're a painter, sculptor, or printmaker, showcasing your work to the world can be a daunting task. Here are 8 actionable strategies to help graduating seniors of LSAA display their art:

1. **Build a Strong Online Presence**: In today's digital age, an online presence is essential for artists. Create a professional website or portfolio to showcase your work, and engage with your audience on social media platforms.

2. **Network and Collaborate**: Networking with fellow artists, curators, and galleries can lead to exciting opportunities and collaborations. Attend local art events, join art organizations, and engage in online communities to expand your network.

3. **Develop a Unique Brand**: Establishing a unique brand identity can help you stand out in a crowded art market. Experiment with different styles, themes, and techniques to develop a distinctive voice.

4. **Create Curated Collections**: Curating collections of your work can help you showcase your artistic range and versatility. Consider creating thematic exhibitions or series that demonstrate your artistic growth.

5. **Engage with Local Art Communities**: Connecting with local art communities can lead to exhibition opportunities, commissions, and collaborations. Engage with local artists, galleries, and art organizations to build relationships and expand your network.

6. **Develop a Pricing Strategy**: Pricing your artwork can be a challenging task, especially for emerging artists. Research the market, consider your materials and time, and price your work competitively.

7. **Learn About Art Law and Contracts**: Understanding art law and contracts can help you protect your intellectual property and negotiate successful business transactions. Research local and national regulations, and consult with an attorney to ensure your contracts are comprehensive.

8. **Stay Up-to-Date with Industry Trends**: Staying current with industry trends and technologies can help you stay ahead of the competition. Attend workshops, webinars, and conferences to learn about new materials, techniques, and marketing strategies.
