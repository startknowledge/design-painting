Why This Mural Opportunity Is a Big Deal for Indiana Artists

Before we explore how to win the commission, it’s important to understand why this project is significant.

1. The Fever’s Growing Influence in Indiana

The Indiana Fever have been a cornerstone of women’s basketball since their founding in 2000. With a record-breaking 2023 season (finishing 4th in the WNBA), they’ve solidified their place as a major sports franchise in Indiana.

  • 2023 Attendance: Over 1.2 million fans attended Fever games.
  • Community Impact: The team runs youth basketball programs and women’s empowerment initiatives.
  • New Facility: The Player Performance Center (expected to open in 2024) will include state-of-the-art training facilities, locker rooms, and fan engagement zones—making it a hub for basketball culture in the state.

A mural here won’t just be decorative—it will be a symbol of Indiana’s basketball legacy and a source of pride for players, coaches, and fans.

2. The Power of Murals in Public & Sports Spaces

Murals in sports facilities serve multiple purposes:

  • Inspiration: Athletes often look at murals before games to channel motivation.
  • Branding: They reinforce the team’s identity and create a unique atmosphere.
  • Community Connection: Murals can celebrate local culture, making visitors feel more connected to the space.
  • Artistic Legacy: A well-designed mural can elevate an artist’s portfolio and attract future clients.

Example: The Los Angeles Lakers’ "Mamba Mentality" mural in their training facility is a legendary piece that embodies the team’s competitive spirit. Similarly, the Boston Celtics’ "Celts Pride" mural in TD Garden is a fan favorite, blending history, culture, and basketball.

For Indiana, this mural could combine local heritage, women’s basketball history, and modern art—making it uniquely Indiana.

3. Why Local Artists Should Apply

The Fever have expressed a preference for hiring a Central Indiana artist to ensure the mural reflects the local community’s voice. This means: ✔ No need to compete with out-of-state artists (reducing competition). ✔ A chance to work with a professional sports team (great for networking). ✔ Potential for repeat commissions (if the mural is successful, the Fever may commission more art in the future).

But how do you make sure your proposal stands out? Let’s explore 8 key strategies to maximize your chances.

8 Actionable Strategies to Win the Indiana Fever Mural Commission

If you’re a Central Indiana artist looking to apply, you need more than just talent—you need a strategic approach. Here’s how to position yourself as the best candidate.

Strategy 1: Research the Fever’s Brand & Values

Before pitching your idea, deeply understand what the Fever represents.

Key Research Areas:

  • Team History: The Fever were founded in 2000 as an expansion team. Their first WNBA championship came in 2020, making them a rising powerhouse.
  • Player Stories: Highlight local players (like Kelsey Plum, a Fever legend) or current stars (like A’ja Wilson, who played for Indiana before moving to Las Vegas).
  • Community Focus: The team runs youth basketball programs and women’s empowerment initiatives. Your mural should reflect these values.
  • Fan Culture: Indiana fans are passionate—think Hoosier pride, basketball traditions, and a love for underdog stories.

How to Apply This:

  • Mention specific Fever moments in your proposal (e.g., "I’d love to incorporate the 2020 championship moment where the team celebrated in downtown Indianapolis").
  • Show familiarity with Indiana culture (e.g., references to Hoosier basketball history, local landmarks, or Indiana’s artistic traditions).

Example Proposal Hook: "I’d love to design a mural that celebrates Indiana’s basketball legacy while honoring the Fever’s journey—perhaps featuring a modern take on the ‘Hoosier Hysteria’ spirit, blending retro WNBA aesthetics with contemporary street art elements."

Strategy 2: Propose a Mural Concept That Tells a Story

A great mural doesn’t just look good—it tells a story. The Fever’s mural should inspire players, connect with fans, and represent Indiana’s identity.

Storytelling Elements to Include:

✅ Historical Context: Show the evolution of women’s basketball in Indiana. ✅ Player Inspiration: Feature real players (past or present) in a dynamic, motivational pose. ✅ Community Tie-Ins: Incorporate local symbols (e.g., Hoosier Dome, downtown Indianapolis, or Indiana University’s basketball legacy). ✅ Abstract vs. Figurative Balance: If you prefer abstract art, consider symbolic elements (e.g., a ball breaking through chains to represent breaking barriers in women’s sports).

Real-World Example: The "Dream Big" Mural at the University of Kentucky

While not a sports team, the UK basketball facility’s mural features:

  • Historical players (like Dan Issel) in a modern, dynamic style.
  • Symbolic elements (a rising sun representing hope and progress).
  • Local landmarks (the Big Blue Madness spirit woven into the design).

Your mural could do something similar—but for the Fever.

Strategy 3: Showcase Your Technical & Creative Skills

The Fever will want to know:

  • Can you handle a large-scale mural?
  • Do you have experience with public/commercial art?
  • Can you work within a tight deadline?

How to Prove Your Capabilities:

  • Portfolio Highlights: Include murals, large-scale paintings, or public art projects in your proposal.
  • Material Expertise: Mention if you’ve worked with spray paint, acrylic, or fresco techniques (important for durability).
  • Collaboration Experience: If you’ve worked with clients, contractors, or other artists, highlight it.

Example Portfolio Description: "In 2022, I completed a 15-foot-tall mural for the Indianapolis Public Library’s youth center, featuring local children’s stories in a vibrant, interactive style. This project required precise scaling and durable materials, skills I’d bring to the Fever’s performance center."

Strategy 4: Offer a Hybrid Approach (Digital + Physical)

Since the mural will be inside a high-traffic facility, the Fever may want digital elements (like projected visuals or interactive components).

Ways to Enhance Your Proposal:

  • Augmented Reality (AR) Integration: Some modern murals use AR to bring the artwork to life (e.g., players "appearing" when viewed through a phone).
  • Interactive Elements: Could the mural change seasonally (e.g., holiday themes, player spotlights)?
  • Digital Mockups: Provide a high-resolution digital rendering so the Fever can visualize the final product before committing.

Example: "I’d love to propose a hybrid mural—a traditional painted piece with embedded LED lights that can highlight key elements during games. This would create a dynamic, ever-changing atmosphere that evolves with the team’s seasons."

Strategy 5: Align with the Fever’s Social Media & Marketing Goals

The Fever have a strong social media presence (over 500K followers on Instagram). A mural that works well online could boost engagement.

How to Make Your Mural Instagram-Friendly:

  • High-Contrast Colors: Bright, vibrant hues (like electric blue, fiery red, or golden yellow) perform well in photos.
  • Symbolic Imagery: Elements like a rising sun, a broken chain, or a basketball mid-air are visually striking in social media posts.
  • Player Cameos: If possible, include a subtle nod to current players (e.g., A’ja Wilson’s signature moves).

Example: "I’d design a mural with golden accents (representing the Fever’s 2020 championship) and dynamic basketball imagery, ensuring it pops in photos—perfect for the team’s #FeverFamily social media campaigns."

Strategy 6: Propose a Collaborative Process

The Fever may want input from players, coaches, or fans. Show that you’re open to feedback and willing to refine your design.

How to Structure Collaboration:

  • Initial Sketches: Send 3-5 concept sketches for feedback.
  • Player Input: Offer to meet with players to discuss what inspires them.
  • Fan Surveys: Suggest a quick poll to see what local fans would love to see in the mural.

Example Proposal: "I’d love to collaborate with the Fever’s players to ensure the mural resonates with them. For example, we could host a quick sketch session where players draw their own basketball memories, which I’d weave into the final design."

Strategy 7: Highlight Sustainability & Durability

Since the mural will be in a high-traffic area, it needs to last for years.

Sustainable & Durable Options:

  • Eco-Friendly Paints: Use low-VOC, non-toxic paints (important for indoor air quality).
  • UV-Resistant Materials: Prevents fading over time.
  • Modular Design: If possible, sections that can be updated (e.g., player spotlights).

Example: "I’d use 100% eco-friendly, UV-resistant acrylic paints to ensure the mural remains vibrant for decades, while also minimizing environmental impact."

Strategy 8: Include a Clear Timeline & Budget Breakdown

The Fever will want to know:

  • How long will the project take?
  • What’s the cost breakdown?
  • Are there any hidden fees?

How to Present This Professionally:

  • Phase 1 (Research & Sketches): 2-4 weeks
  • Phase 2 (Final Design & Approval): 2 weeks
  • Phase 3 (Execution): 4-6 weeks (depending on size)
  • Phase 4 (Touch-Ups & Handover): 1 week

Budget Example:

Design & Sketches $1,500
