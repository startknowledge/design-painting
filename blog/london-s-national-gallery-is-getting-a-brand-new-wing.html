London's National Gallery is Getting a Brand New Wing: What This Means for Art Lovers and Design Enthusiasts

The world of art and design is abuzz with the latest news: London's National Gallery is getting a brand new wing. In fact, according to a report by the Art Fund in 2019, the National Gallery is one of the top 5 most visited museums in the UK, with a significant impact on the country's tourism industry. With the new wing, the gallery is expected to attract even more visitors, with projections suggesting an increase of up to 10% in the next year (Source: Visit London, 2022).

Introduction to the New Wing

The new wing, designed by renowned architect Annabelle Selldorf, will provide an additional 20,000 square feet of exhibition space, allowing the gallery to showcase even more of its extensive collection. The wing will feature state-of-the-art facilities, including advanced climate control systems and cutting-edge lighting technology, ensuring that the artworks are preserved and displayed in the best possible conditions. With a focus on sustainability, the new wing will also incorporate green roofs, solar panels, and a rainwater harvesting system, making it an exemplary model of eco-friendly design. The construction process is expected to be completed by 2025, with the new wing opening to the public in early 2026.
