  • Collaborate with other experts: Collaborating with other experts, such as art historians and conservators, can provide valuable insights and expertise. This can help to ensure that the restoration is accurate and effective.
  • Test and evaluate the restoration: Finally, it is essential to test and evaluate the restoration, to ensure that it is effective and durable. This can involve a range of techniques, including visual examination and scientific analysis.
  • Consider the long-term preservation of the canvas: The long-term preservation of the canvas is critical, as it will help to ensure that the artwork remains in good condition for generations to come. This can involve a range of strategies, including climate control and storage.
  • Develop a maintenance plan: Developing a maintenance plan is essential, as it will help to ensure that the canvas is regularly inspected and maintained. This can help to prevent damage and ensure that the artwork remains in good condition.
