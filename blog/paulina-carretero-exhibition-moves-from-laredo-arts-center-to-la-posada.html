Common Mistakes in Exhibition Relocations (And How to Avoid Them)

1. Underestimating Logistics

Mistake: Failing to plan for shipping, installation, or legal contracts.

How to Avoid:

  • Hire a professional art mover (e.g., Art Handling Services).
  • Review gallery contracts for royalty splits, insurance, and cancellation clauses.
  • Test tech setup (Wi-Fi, sound, lighting) before the opening.

2. Ignoring the Local Audience

Mistake: Assuming new gallery = new audience without engaging locals.

How to Avoid:

  • Host a "meet the artist" event in the new city’s art districts.
  • Partner with local schools for art education programs.
  • Offer discounts to first-time buyers in the new location.

3. Overlooking Digital Presence

Mistake: Not updating websites, social media, or SEO after the move.

How to Avoid:

  • Redirect old URLs to the new gallery page.
  • Update Google My Business with new location details.
  • Run a "Grand Reopening" campaign on all platforms.

4. Setting Unrealistic Sales Goals

Mistake: Expecting immediate success without marketing or networking.

How to Avoid:

  • Set incremental goals (e.g., "First month: 20 sales, then scale.").
  • Track KPIs (website traffic, social engagement, foot traffic).
  • Adjust strategies based on real-time data.

5. Not Securing Backup Plans

Mistake: Assuming the new gallery will handle everything.

How to Avoid:

  • Have a "Plan B" venue in case of last-minute cancellations.
  • Keep a small inventory in a secure storage unit.
  • Negotiate flexible contracts (e.g., early termination clauses).
