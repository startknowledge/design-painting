10 Actionable Strategies for Enhancing Your South Side Neighbor's Front

To help you get started, we've compiled a list of 10 actionable strategies for enhancing your south side neighbor's front:

  • Harmonize Color Schemes: Choose a color palette that complements your neighbor's exterior design, creating a sense of continuity and cohesion.
  • Balance Symmetry and Asymmetry: Strike a balance between symmetrical and asymmetrical elements to add visual interest and depth to the exterior design.
  • Incorporate Outdoor Lighting: Strategically place outdoor lighting fixtures to highlight your neighbor's architectural features and create a warm ambiance.
  • Add Textured Elements: Incorporate textured elements, such as plants, stone, or brick, to add depth and visual interest to the exterior design.
  • Define the Entrance: Create a clear and inviting entrance by using a statement piece, such as a bold-colored door or a decorative archway.
  • Incorporate Vertical Elements: Use vertical elements, such as trellises or arbors, to add height and create a sense of layering.
  • Consider the Neighborhood Context: Take into account the surrounding neighborhood and incorporate design elements that reflect the local style and character.
  • Don't Forget the Details: Pay attention to the small details, such as house numbers, mailboxes, and door hardware, to create a polished and finished look.
  • Create a Focal Point: Designate a focal point, such as a statement piece of art or a decorative fountain, to draw the eye and create visual interest.
  • Maintain a Consistent Style: Ensure that the exterior design elements are consistent with the overall style and aesthetic of your neighbor's property.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

When it comes to exterior design, there are several common mistakes to avoid. One of the most significant mistakes is ignoring the neighborhood context. Failing to consider the surrounding neighborhood and incorporating design elements that clash with the local style can result in a disjointed and uninviting exterior. Another mistake is overlooking the details. Neglecting to pay attention to the small details, such as house numbers and door hardware, can lead to a polished but unfinished look. By being aware of these common mistakes, you can avoid them and create a beautiful and functional exterior design that enhances your south side neighbor's front.
