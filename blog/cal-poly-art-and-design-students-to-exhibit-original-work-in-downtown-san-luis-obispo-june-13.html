Actionable Strategies for Art and Design Students

To make the most of this opportunity, here are 10 actionable strategies or tips for Cal Poly art and design students:

  • Develop a strong online presence: Create a professional website and social media profiles to showcase your work and connect with potential buyers.
  • Build a portfolio: Create a physical or digital portfolio that highlights your best work and demonstrates your skills and versatility as an artist.
  • Network and collaborate: Attend art events and join online communities to connect with other artists, collectors, and industry professionals.
  • Stay up-to-date with industry trends: Follow art and design blogs, attend workshops and conferences, and participate in online forums to stay informed about the latest developments in the industry.
  • Develop a unique style: Experiment with different techniques and mediums to develop a unique and recognizable style that sets you apart from other artists.
  • Learn about the business side of art: Take courses or attend workshops on art marketing, pricing, and sales to learn how to successfully sell your work.
  • Prepare for the exhibition: Make sure you have a clear and concise artist statement, business cards, and a plan for displaying and selling your work.
  • Follow up with attendees: After the exhibition, follow up with attendees who expressed interest in your work to thank them for their support and provide them with more information about your art.
  • Continuously create new work: Keep creating new and innovative pieces to stay relevant and attract new fans and collectors.
  • Stay organized and focused: Use tools like calendars and to-do lists to stay organized and focused, and make sure you meet deadlines and follow through on commitments.
