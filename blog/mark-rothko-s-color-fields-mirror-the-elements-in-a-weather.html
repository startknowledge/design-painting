Introduction to Mark Rothko’s Color Fields

Mark Rothko’s color field paintings are characterized by large, rectangular fields of color that seem to hover and vibrate on the canvas. Rothko’s use of color is not just about aesthetics; it’s about creating an immersive experience that draws the viewer into the depths of the painting.
