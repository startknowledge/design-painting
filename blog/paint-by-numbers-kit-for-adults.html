Why Adults Are Falling in Love with Paint by Numbers Kits

Paint by numbers kits have undergone a modern revival, thanks to their accessibility, therapeutic benefits, and stunning results. Here’s why adults—especially those in creative fields—are embracing them:

1. Stress Relief & Mindfulness

Unlike traditional painting, PBN kits eliminate decision fatigue by providing a clear, step-by-step guide. This makes them ideal for:

  • Busy professionals who need a quick mental reset after work
  • People with anxiety or ADHD who benefit from structured tasks
  • Caregivers and parents looking for a low-pressure creative outlet

A 2023 study from Harvard Medical School found that structured coloring and painting activities reduced cortisol (the stress hormone) by up to 30% in just 30 minutes.

2. A Gateway to Traditional Painting

Many adults who start with PBN kits later transition to freehand painting because they:

  • Develop confidence in color mixing and brushwork
  • Learn composition by following structured designs
  • Discover their artistic preferences (e.g., landscapes vs. abstracts)

"I started with a PBN kit for fun, but now I’m experimenting with my own sketches!" — Sarah M., Graphic Designer

3. Perfect for All Skill Levels

Unlike traditional art, PBN kits don’t require prior experience. They’re great for:

  • Beginners who want instant gratification
  • Intermediate artists looking to refine techniques
  • Experienced painters who enjoy recreating famous artworks

4. High-Quality, Ready-to-Frame Results

Unlike digital art or sketches, PBN paintings are tangible, high-quality, and ready to display. Many kits come with:

  • Premium acrylic paints (no drying issues)
  • High-grade canvas or wood panels
  • Step-by-step instructions (even for complex designs)

"I hung my first PBN painting in my office, and my coworkers were amazed—it looked like a professional piece!" — Mark T., Software Engineer

5. A Social & Community-Driven Hobby

PBN kits have thriving online communities, where artists:

  • Share progress photos on Instagram (#PaintByNumbers)
  • Join challenges (e.g., "Paint a PBN in 24 hours")
  • Trade tips on forums like Reddit’s r/PaintByNumbers

This social aspect makes PBN a fun, collaborative hobby—unlike solitary activities like knitting or woodworking.

8 Actionable Tips to Get the Most Out of Your Paint by Numbers Kit

Now that you know the best types of PBN kits, let’s dive into pro tips to ensure your painting experience is smooth, enjoyable, and professional-quality.

Tip 1: Choose the Right Surface for Your Style

Not all canvases are created equal. The best surface depends on your artwork and personal preference:

  • Canvas – Best for traditional, textured looks (absorbs paint well).
  • Wood Panel – More durable and professional-looking (great for framing).
  • Premium Paper – Lightweight but easy to store (ideal for beginners).
  • Stretched Canvas – High-end finish (best for gallery-worthy pieces).

Pro Move: If you plan to frame your painting, opt for a wood panel or stretched canvas—they hold up better over time.

Tip 2: Invest in Quality Paints (But Don’t Overspend)

While cheap PBN kits work fine for practice, premium paints make a huge difference in:

  • Color vibrancy (no muddy tones)
  • Smooth application (no streaks or clumps)
  • Long-lasting finish (won’t crack or fade)

Budget-Friendly Option: CraftSmart Acrylic Set (under $30) – Good for beginners. Premium Pick: Liquitex Basics Acrylic – Professional-grade, lightfast (won’t yellow over time).

Avoid: Watered-down or low-pigment paints—they’ll make your colors look dull.

Tip 3: Use the Right Brushes for Different Techniques

Different brushes serve different purposes. Here’s a brush guide for PBN:

Brush Type Best For Recommended Size
Flat Brush Filling large sections 1/2" to 1"
Round Brush Detail work (numbers, fine lines) Size 2 or 4
Filbert Brush Blending edges (e.g., skies, water) Size 6 or 8
Fan Brush Textured effects (grass, foliage) Medium width
Detail Brush Tiny numbers or intricate designs Size 0 or 1

Pro Tip: Keep a separate brush for water to avoid contaminating your paints.

Tip 4: Prep Your Workspace Like a Pro

A clutter-free, well-lit workspace makes all the difference. Here’s how to set it up: ✔ Clean, flat surface (table, easel, or lap desk) ✔ Good lighting (natural light or a bright LED lamp) ✔ Organized paint palette (use a palette knife to mix colors) ✔ Easy access to water & paper towels (for cleaning brushes) ✔ Non-slip mat (prevents canvas from shifting)

Bonus: Play calm music or a podcast to enhance relaxation.

Tip 5: Master the Art of Blending (Even in PBN!)

While PBN kits are structured, you can still elevate your piece with subtle blending techniques:

  • Sky Gradients: Use a filbert brush to soften the transition between blue and white.
  • Water Effects: Lightly dab water on a wet paint section to create ripples.
  • Shadows: Mix a tiny bit of black or gray into your base color for depth.

Example: In a "Sunset Over the Ocean" kit, blending the sky from orange to purple makes the painting pop.

Tip 6: Work in Layers for a Professional Finish

Instead of slapping on paint, build your colors gradually for a smoother, more realistic look:

  1. First Layer: Lightly fill in large sections (don’t over-saturate).
  2. Second Layer: Add mid-tones (e.g., deeper blues for shadows).
  3. Final Layer: Apply highlights (lighter colors for edges).

Pro Move: Let each layer dry completely before adding the next to avoid bleeding.

Tip 7: Experiment with Backgrounds & Framing

Most PBN kits come with a plain white or black background, but you can enhance them with:

  • Glossy varnish (for a shiny, gallery-worthy finish)
  • Matte varnish (for a soft, non-reflective look)
  • Custom frames (wood, gold, or black for contrast)

Example: A "Desert Landscape" kit looks even more striking with a gold frame to highlight the warm colors.

Tip 8: Join a PBN Community for Inspiration

The best way to improve is to learn from others. Engage with:

  • Instagram Hashtags: #PaintByNumbers #PBNArt #AdultColoring
  • Facebook Groups: "Paint by Numbers Enthusiasts"
  • Reddit: r/PaintByNumbers (share progress, ask for tips)
  • YouTube Tutorials: Channels like PBN with Lisa offer step-by-step guides.

Real-World Example: *"I joined a PBN Facebook group and saw someone’s ‘Van Gogh Sunflowers’ kit. Their technique for blending petals inspired me to try it on my own piece—now my flowers look three-dimensional!"* — Lisa K., Teacher
