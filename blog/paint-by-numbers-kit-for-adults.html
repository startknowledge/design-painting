How to Choose the Best Paint by Numbers Kit for Adults

Not all paint by numbers kits are created equal. To get the best experience, consider these key factors:

1. Size & Complexity

  • Small kits (8"x10"–12"x16") – Best for quick projects, gifts, or beginners.
  • Medium kits (16"x20"–24"x36") – Ideal for wall art, skill-building, and intermediate painters.
  • Large kits (36"x48"+) – Perfect for serious hobbyists, murals, or those who love detail work.

Pro tip: If you’re new, start with a medium-sized kit—it’s big enough to feel rewarding but small enough to finish in a weekend.

2. Number of Colors & Paint Quality

  • Basic kits (30–50 colors) – Good for budget-friendly beginners.
  • Premium kits (80–120+ colors) – Better for realistic details and depth.
  • High-quality paints (acrylic or oil-based) last longer and blend smoother.

Real-world example: A budget kit with 30 colors might look fine at first glance, but the paints dry unevenly, and the final piece lacks vibrancy. In contrast, a premium kit with 100+ colors (like those from Chevrolet Paint by Numbers) produces professional-grade results with richer hues and smoother transitions.

3. Design Style (What Do You Want to Paint?)

Paint by numbers kits come in endless themes, including:

  • Landscapes (mountains, forests, oceans)
  • Animals & Wildlife (dogs, cats, elephants, birds)
  • Abstract & Modern Art (geometric patterns, minimalist designs)
  • Pop Culture & Celebrities (Disney characters, superheroes, famous paintings)
  • Religious & Spiritual (mandalas, biblical scenes, zen art)

Real-world example: A history buff might choose a kit featuring the Mona Lisa or Van Gogh’s Starry Night, while a dog lover could opt for a realistic golden retriever portrait. The right design keeps you motivated—so pick something you’ll enjoy looking at for years.

4. Canvas Material & Finish

  • Canvas – Durable, textured, and great for long-term display.
  • Wooden panels – Sturdier but can be heavier to hang.
  • Acrylic or plastic – Lightweight but may yellow over time.

Pro tip: If you plan to frame your painting, choose a canvas with a gesso primer—it ensures colors stay vibrant and prevents warping.

5. Brand Reputation & Customer Reviews

Not all brands are equal. Top-rated adult paint by numbers brands include:

Real-world example: A beginner painter bought a cheap kit from an unknown brand and was disappointed—the paints bleeded into each other, and the canvas cracked after a few months. Switching to Chevrolet’s premium line made a world of difference in color accuracy and durability.

10 Actionable Tips to Get the Most Out of Your Paint by Numbers Kit

Now that you’ve picked the perfect kit, here’s how to maximize your experience:

1. Prep Your Workspace Like a Pro

A clutter-free, well-lit area makes painting much more enjoyable. ✅ Use a drop cloth (or old newspapers) to protect your table. ✅ Work near a window (natural light helps with color accuracy). ✅ Keep your paints in a palette (or use a plastic tray) to avoid spills. ✅ Have a damp cloth ready for brush cleaning.

Real-world example: A painter named Lisa used to spill paint everywhere because she worked on her kitchen table. After setting up a dedicated painting station with a cloth-covered table and good lighting, she noticed her work became cleaner and more precise.

2. Start with the Right Brushes

Not all brushes are created equal. Invest in a few key brushes for different techniques:

  • Fine detail brush (size 0 or 1) – For small numbers and intricate areas.
  • Medium round brush (size 4–6) – Best for large sections and blending.
  • Flat brush (size 8–10) – Great for filling in big areas quickly.

Pro tip: Avoid cheap brushes—they shed bristles and ruin your painting. Brands like Princeton, Da Vinci, or Royal & Langnick are worth the investment.

3. Load Your Brush Correctly

How you pick up paint affects smoothness and control. ✅ Dip the brush halfway (not the tip or the ferrule). ✅ Tap off excess paint on the palette edge (not the canvas). ✅ Use a "dab and drag" technique for even strokes.

Real-world example: A new painter named Jake struggled with uneven strokes because he was loading too much paint. After watching a YouTube tutorial on brush technique, he improved dramatically—his lines became smoother, and his colors blended better.

4. Work in Layers for a Professional Finish

Instead of flooding a section with paint, build up thin, even layers. ✅ Start with a light base coat (let it dry slightly). ✅ Add a second layer for more intensity. ✅ Use a dry brush for subtle details (like tree bark or fur).

Real-world example: A landscape kit looked flat and dull at first. After applying two thin layers (instead of one thick one), the sky and water took on depth and realism.

5. Blend Colors for a Seamless Look

Paint by numbers numbers are just a guide—don’t be afraid to blend colors for a more natural effect. ✅ Use a damp brush to soften edges between sections. ✅ Mix paints slightly where two colors meet (e.g., sky transitioning to clouds). ✅ Work in small sections to prevent drying lines.

Real-world example: A painter named Mia noticed that her mountain kit looked too rigid because she followed the numbers too strictly. After softening the edges between green and brown, her **landscape took on a more realistic, three-dimensional appearance.

6. Let Each Section Dry Completely

Rushing can ruin your painting—patience is key. ✅ Use a fan or open window for faster drying (if needed). ✅ Avoid touching wet paint—it can smudge and bleed. ✅ Check the paint can for drying time (acrylics dry in 10–30 minutes; oils take hours).

Real-world example: A beginner named Tom tried to speed up his painting by working on multiple sections at once. The result? Colors bled together, and his final piece looked messy. After waiting for each section to dry, his work turned out flawless.

7. Experiment with Backgrounds & Framing

Once your painting is fully dry, you can enhance it further: ✅ Add a shadow box for depth. ✅ **Use a floating frame to make it pop off the wall. ✅ **Spray it with a matte or gloss varnish for extra protection.

Real-world example: A painter named Priya framed her finished kit in a simple black frame—but after seeing online galleries, she realized white or wood frames would highlight the colors better. She re-framed it and was surprised by the difference.

8. Turn It Into a Long-Term Hobby

Paint by numbers is just the beginning—here’s how to take your skills further: ✅ **Try freehand painting after mastering the basics. ✅ Join an online community (like r/paintbynumbers on Reddit). ✅ **Experiment with different mediums (watercolor, pastels, digital art). ✅ Sell your finished pieces on Etsy, eBay, or local markets.

Real-world example: A former office worker named Alex started with paint by numbers as a stress reliever. After completing 20+ kits, he enrolled in an online art course and now sells his original paintings—some of which resemble his early paint by numbers designs.

9. Use the Right Tools for Touch-Ups

Mistakes happen—don’t panic! Here’s how to fix them: ✅ For smudges: Use a damp brush to lift off excess paint. ✅ For missed spots: Reapply paint carefully (let it dry between layers). ✅ For color errors: Cover with a thin layer of the correct paint.

Real-world example: A painter named Emma accidentally painted over a number with the wrong color. Instead of giving up, she covered it with a thin layer of the correct paint and blended it in. The result was so seamless that no one noticed the mistake!
