10 Actionable Strategies from ELLE Decor’s 2026 A-List

So, what can you learn from ELLE Decor's 2026 A-List? Here are 10 actionable strategies to help you improve your interior design skills:

  • Start with a neutral base: Many of the designers on the A-List emphasize the importance of starting with a neutral base, such as a white or gray wall, and then adding pops of color and texture through furniture and accessories.
  • Experiment with different materials: Don't be afraid to mix and match different materials, such as wood, metal, and glass, to add depth and visual interest to your space.
  • Consider the 60-30-10 rule: This rule suggests that 60% of your space should be a dominant color, 30% a secondary color, and 10% an accent color. This can help create a balanced and harmonious color scheme.
  • Pay attention to lighting: Lighting can make or break a space, so it's essential to consider the type of lighting you need, whether it's overhead lighting, table lamps, or floor lamps.
  • Don't forget about texture: Texture can add depth and visual interest to your space, so don't be afraid to mix and match different textures, such as velvet, linen, and wood.
  • Bring in plants: Plants are not only good for air quality, but they can also add a touch of natural beauty to your space.
  • Create a focal point: Every room needs a focal point, whether it's a statement piece of furniture, a work of art, or a stunning view.
  • Consider the flow of your space: Think about how you want to move through your space and how you can create a sense of flow and circulation.
  • Don’t be afraid to take risks: Interior design is all about taking risks and trying new things, so don’t be afraid to experiment and try out new ideas.
  • Seek inspiration from nature: Nature is a great source of inspiration for interior design, so take a walk outside and see what you can learn from the natural world.

Common Mistakes to Avoid in Interior Design

While studying the work of ELLE Decor's 2026 A-List designers can provide valuable insights and inspiration, it's also important to be aware of common mistakes to avoid in interior design. Here are a few mistakes to watch out for:

  • Not considering the flow of your space: A well-designed space should have a sense of flow and circulation, so make sure to think about how you want to move through your space and how you can create a sense of flow.
  • Not paying attention to scale: Scale is essential in interior design, so make sure to choose furniture and accessories that are proportional to your space.
  • Not considering the natural light: Natural light can make or break a space, so make sure to consider the natural light in your space and how you can use it to your advantage.
  • Not being true to your personal style: Your space should reflect your personal style and aesthetic, so don't be afraid to add your own unique touch and personality to your design.
