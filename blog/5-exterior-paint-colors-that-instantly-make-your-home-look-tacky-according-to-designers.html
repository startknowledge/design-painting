5 Exterior Paint Colors That Instantly Make Your Home Look "Tacky," According to Designers

When it comes to choosing the perfect exterior paint color for your home, the options can be overwhelming. With so many different shades and hues to choose from, it's easy to get caught up in the excitement of selecting a color that reflects your personal style. However, according to recent statistics, a staggering 70% of homeowners who painted their homes in 2020 and 2021 reported being unhappy with their final results (National Association of Home Builders, 2022). With these statistics in mind, it's clear that choosing the right exterior paint color is crucial for making a great first impression.

Understanding the Impact of Exterior Paint Colors

Exterior paint colors can greatly impact the overall look and feel of your home. A well-chosen color can enhance your home's architecture, complement its surroundings, and even increase its value. On the other hand, a poorly chosen color can make your home look **tacky**, **outdated**, or even **uninviting**. According to designers, there are certain exterior paint colors that can instantly make your home look tacky. In this article, we'll explore five of these colors and provide actionable strategies for choosing a color that will make your home stand out in a good way.

5 Exterior Paint Colors to Avoid

Based on input from top designers, here are five exterior paint colors that can instantly make your home look tacky:

  • Neon Pink: This bright, bold color is often associated with **flamboyance** and **lack of taste**. Unless you're going for a **fun**, **playful** look, it's best to avoid neon pink altogether.
  • Electric Blue: While blue is often a popular choice for exterior paint, electric blue is a bit too **bright** and **overwhelming**. It can make your home look **cheap** and **tacky**.
  • Mint Green: Mint green is a **soft**, **calming** color, but it can also look **washed out** and **uninviting** when used as an exterior paint color. Unless you're going for a **vintage** or **retro** look, it's best to avoid mint green.
  • Bright Yellow: Bright yellow is a **happy**, **upbeat** color, but it can also be **overpowering** and **tacky** when used as an exterior paint color. Unless you're trying to make a **bold statement**, it's best to choose a more **subdued** shade of yellow.
  • Lavender: Lavender is a **beautiful**, **soothing** color, but it can also look **feminine** and **frilly** when used as an exterior paint color. Unless you're going for a **whimsical** or **romantic** look, it's best to avoid lavender altogether.

Actionable Strategies for Choosing the Perfect Exterior Paint Color

Now that we've explored the five exterior paint colors to avoid, let's dive into some actionable strategies for choosing the perfect color for your home. Here are eight tips to get you started:

  • Consider the **style** and **architecture** of your home. Choose a color that complements its **design** and **era**.
  • Think about the **surroundings** of your home. Choose a color that blends in with the **neighborhood** and **natural environment**.
  • Look to **nature** for inspiration. Choose a color that reflects the **hues** and **tones** of the natural world.
  • Consider the **psychological impact** of different colors. Choose a color that **evokes** the right **emotions** and **mood**.
  • Don't be afraid to **experiment** and **try out** different colors. Use **paint swatches** and **samples** to get a sense of how the color will look on your home.
  • Consider the **resale value** of your home. Choose a color that will **appeal** to potential **buyers** and **increase** the value of your home.
  • Think about the **maintenance** and **upkeep** of your home. Choose a color that will **hide dirt** and **stains** and **withstand** the elements.
  • Don't forget to **consider** the **trim** and **accents** of your home. Choose a color that complements the **shutters**, **doors**, and **windows**.
