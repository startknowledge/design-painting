She Came for Design and Stayed for Art: How to Turn Your Passion into a Career

As of 2022, the design industry has experienced significant growth, with the global market size projected to reach $162.3 billion by 2025 (Source: Grand View Research, 2020). This surge in demand has led to an increase in individuals pursuing careers in design, with many discovering that their passion for design can also lead them to a love for art. In fact, a survey conducted by the American Institute of Graphic Design found that 71% of designers reported having a strong interest in art, with 45% stating that they had considered pursuing a career in fine arts at some point (AIGA, 2019). If you're one of the many individuals who have discovered a passion for art through design, you're not alone. In this article, we'll explore how you can turn your passion into a career, and provide actionable strategies for success.

Introduction to the World of Art and Design

The lines between art and design have long been blurred, with many designers finding inspiration in the world of fine arts. As a designer, you may have started out with a focus on creating functional and aesthetically pleasing designs, but found yourself drawn to the creative expression and emotional resonance of art. This is a common phenomenon, with many designers discovering that their passion for design can also lead them to a love of art. By embracing this intersection of art and design, you can unlock new creative possibilities and turn your passion into a fulfilling career.

Strategies for Turning Your Passion into a Career

So, how can you turn your passion for art and design into a career? Here are 8-10 actionable strategies to get you started:

  • Develop Your Skills: As a designer, you already have a strong foundation in visual communication and creative problem-solving. To transition into a career in art, focus on developing your skills in areas such as drawing, painting, and sculpture. Consider taking classes or workshops to learn new techniques and mediums.
  • Build a Portfolio: A strong portfolio is essential for any artist or designer. Create a body of work that showcases your style, technique, and creative vision. Include a mix of traditional and digital media, and be sure to highlight your best pieces.
  • Network and Collaborate: The art and design communities are all about connection and collaboration. Attend exhibitions, join online forums and social media groups, and participate in local art events. By building relationships with other artists and designers, you can learn about new opportunities, get feedback on your work, and stay inspired.
  • Experiment and Take Risks: Art is all about experimentation and taking risks. Don't be afraid to try new things, even if they don't work out. This is where some of the most innovative and exciting art comes from.
  • Stay Up-to-Date with Industry Trends: The art and design worlds are constantly evolving, with new trends and technologies emerging all the time. Stay current by attending conferences, reading industry publications, and following thought leaders on social media.
  • Find Your Niche: With so many talented artists and designers out there, it's essential to find your unique voice and style. Experiment with different mediums and techniques until you find what works best for you.
  • Learn About the Business Side of Art: As a professional artist or designer, you'll need to understand the business side of the industry. Learn about marketing, pricing, and sales, and consider taking classes or workshops to develop your business skills.
  • Join Online Communities: Online communities such as Behance, Dribbble, and DeviantArt can provide valuable feedback, inspiration, and networking opportunities. Share your work, participate in discussions, and engage with other artists and designers.
  • Enter Competitions and Exhibitions: Entering competitions and exhibitions can help you gain recognition, build your portfolio, and get your work seen by a wider audience. Research local and national opportunities, and submit your work to as many as you can.
  • Stay Persistent and Patient: Turning your passion into a career takes time, effort, and perseverance. Don't get discouraged by setbacks or rejections – keep working, learning, and pushing yourself to improve.

Real-World Examples of Designers Turned Artists

There are many examples of designers who have successfully transitioned into careers in art. Take, for instance, the story of Paula Scher, a renowned graphic designer who has also had a successful career as a painter. Scher's work combines her expertise in typography and visual communication with her passion for abstract expressionism, resulting in bold, vibrant paintings that have been exhibited in galleries around the world. Another example is Shepard Fairey, a graphic designer and artist who has become known for his street art and propaganda-style posters. Fairey's work combines his design skills with his passion for social justice and activism, resulting in powerful, thought-provoking art that has been exhibited in museums and galleries worldwide.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

While turning your passion into a career can be exciting, there are also common mistakes to avoid. Here are a few:

  • Don't Be Afraid to Fail: Many designers and artists are perfectionists, but the truth is that failure is an essential part of the creative process. Don't be afraid to take risks and try new things – it's often where the best art comes from.
  • Don't Compare Yourself to Others: It's easy to get caught up in comparing your work to that of other artists and designers. However, this can be detrimental to your creative process and overall well-being. Focus on your own unique voice and style, and celebrate the successes of others.
  • Don't Underprice Your Work: As a professional artist or designer, it's essential to price your work fairly and competitively. Research the market, consider your costs and time, and don't be afraid to charge what you're worth.
  • Don't Forget to Take Care of Yourself: The creative process can be intense and all-consuming. Don't forget to take care of your physical and mental health, and make time for self-care and relaxation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Here are some frequently asked questions about turning your passion for design into a career in art:

What skills do I need to become a successful artist?
To become a successful artist, you'll need to develop a range of skills, including technical proficiency in your chosen medium, creative vision, and business acumen. Consider taking classes or workshops to learn new techniques and develop your skills.
How do I get my work seen by a wider audience?
There are many ways to get your work seen by a wider audience, including entering competitions and exhibitions, joining online communities, and participating in local art events. Consider building a strong online presence, including a website and social media profiles, to showcase your work and connect with potential fans and collectors.
What are some common mistakes to avoid as an artist?
Common mistakes to avoid as an artist include underpricing your work, comparing yourself to others, and forgetting to take care of your physical and mental health. Remember to stay focused on your unique voice and style, and prioritize your well-being and creativity.
How do I stay inspired and motivated as an artist?
To stay inspired and motivated as an artist, consider setting aside time each day or week to experiment and play. Try new techniques, explore different mediums, and seek out new sources of inspiration, such as nature, music, or literature. Remember to take breaks and practice self-care, and don't be afraid to take risks and try new things.
What are some resources for learning more about art and design?
There are many resources available for learning more about art and design, including online courses and tutorials, books and magazines, and local art schools and workshops. Consider checking out websites such as Skillshare, Coursera, and LinkedIn Learning, or following artists and designers on social media to stay inspired and informed.

Conclusion

In conclusion, turning your passion for design into a career in art takes time, effort, and perseverance. By developing your skills, building a portfolio, networking and collaborating, experimenting and taking risks, and staying up-to-date with industry trends, you can unlock new creative possibilities and achieve success as an artist. Remember to stay focused on your unique voice and style, and prioritize your well-being and creativity. With dedication and hard work, you can turn your passion into a fulfilling and rewarding career. So why wait?
