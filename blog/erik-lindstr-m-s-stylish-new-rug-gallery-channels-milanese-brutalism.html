Introduction to Milanese Brutalism

Milanese brutalism is a style that emerged in Milan, Italy, in the 1950s and 1960s. Characterized by the use of raw concrete, exposed brick, and industrial materials, this style is often associated with a sense of ruggedness and simplicity. However, when applied to interior design, Milanese brutalism can also convey a sense of sophistication and elegance. Erik Lindström's rug gallery is a perfect example of how this style can be adapted to create a stylish and modern space.

Actionable Strategies for Incorporating Milanese Brutalism into Your Design

To create a space that channels the essence of Milanese brutalism, consider the following actionable strategies:

  • Use raw materials: Incorporate raw materials such as concrete, brick, and wood into your design to create a sense of ruggedness and authenticity.
  • Emphasize simplicity: Avoid clutter and focus on clean lines and minimal ornamentation to create a sense of simplicity and elegance.
  • Incorporate industrial elements: Add industrial elements such as metal beams, pipes, and ductwork to create a sense of urban grit and sophistication.
  • Play with texture: Mix different textures such as smooth concrete, rough brick, and soft wood to create a sense of depth and visual interest.
  • Use lighting to create ambiance: Use lighting to create a sense of ambiance and mood, such as warm and cozy or cool and industrial.
  • Bring in plants: Add plants to bring in a touch of natural beauty and soften the ruggedness of the space.
  • Create a sense of flow: Use a combination of open and closed spaces to create a sense of flow and visual interest.
  • Incorporate brutalist-inspired artwork: Add artwork and accessories that reflect the brutalist style, such as abstract sculptures and industrial-inspired prints.
  • Experiment with color: Use a bold and muted color palette to create a sense of contrast and visual interest.
  • Pay attention to scale: Pay attention to the scale of the space and the elements within it to create a sense of balance and harmony.

Common Mistakes to Avoid When Incorporating Milanese Brutalism into Your Design

While incorporating Milanese brutalism into your design can be a great way to create a unique and captivating space, there are some common mistakes to avoid. These include:

  • Overusing raw materials: While raw materials such as concrete and brick can be beautiful and authentic, overusing them can create a sense of coldness and sterility.
  • Ignoring the importance of lighting: Lighting can make or break a space, and ignoring its importance can create a sense of darkness and gloom.
  • Forgeting to add texture and interest: A space that is too minimalist and lacking in texture and interest can feel cold and uninviting.
  • Not considering the scale of the space: Failing to consider the scale of the space and the elements within it can create a sense of imbalance and disharmony.
  • Not incorporating plants and natural elements: Failing to incorporate plants and natural elements can create a sense of sterility and lack of warmth.
