Who Was Anni Albers?

Anni Albers was a German-American textile artist, designer, and educator, best known for her pioneering work in modernist design. Born in 1899, Albers began her career as a weaver and later became a prominent figure in the development of modern textile design. Her work was characterized by its simplicity, functionality, and innovative use of materials. Throughout her life, Albers was not afraid to start from zero, experimenting with new techniques, materials, and forms. Her fearless approach to design has inspired generations of artists and designers, and her legacy continues to influence the design industry today.
