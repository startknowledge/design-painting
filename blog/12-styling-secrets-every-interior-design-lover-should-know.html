12 Styling Secrets Every Interior Design Lover Should Know

As an interior design enthusiast, you're likely no stranger to the world of styling and decorating. With the rise of home makeover shows and social media platforms like Instagram and Pinterest, it's easier than ever to get inspired and stay up-to-date on the latest trends. According to a recent survey conducted by the National Kitchen and Bath Association (2022), **71% of homeowners** consider their homes to be a reflection of their personal style. Meanwhile, a report by the American Society of Interior Designers (2020) found that **61% of millennials** are willing to invest in home decor and furniture to create a space that truly feels like their own.

In this blog post, we'll dive into the top 12 styling secrets that every interior design lover should know. From creating a cohesive color palette to incorporating texture and pattern, we'll cover it all. Whether you're a seasoned designer or just starting to explore the world of interior design, these tips and tricks will help you take your styling skills to the next level.

1. Start with a Neutral Base

When it comes to styling a room, it's essential to start with a neutral base. This means choosing a **neutral color palette** for your walls, floors, and furniture. A neutral base provides a clean canvas for adding pops of color and pattern through accessories and decor. For example, a living room with a neutral beige or gray sofa can be easily updated with a bold-colored throw blanket or a patterned rug.

2. Add Texture and Pattern

Texture and pattern are essential elements of any well-styled room. **Mixing different textures**, such as velvet, linen, and wood, can add depth and visual interest to a space. Patterned fabrics, like stripes and florals, can also help to create a cohesive look. Consider adding a **patterned rug** or a **textured throw blanket** to your room to add some visual interest.

3. Consider the 60-30-10 Rule

The 60-30-10 rule is a popular styling secret that can help you create a balanced and harmonious room. The rule states that **60% of the room** should be a dominant color, **30% a secondary color**, and **10% an accent color**. This rule can be applied to any room in the house, from the living room to the bedroom. For example, a bedroom with a dominant blue color (60%) can be paired with a secondary white color (30%) and an accent yellow color (10%) for a cohesive and stylish look.

4. Don't Forget about Lighting

Lighting is a crucial element of any well-styled room. **Layered lighting**, which includes a combination of overhead lighting, table lamps, and floor lamps, can help to create a warm and inviting atmosphere. Consider adding **string lights** or **candles** to your room for a cozy and intimate ambiance.

5. Bring in Plants

Plants are not only good for air quality, but they can also add a touch of style and elegance to a room. **Greenery**, such as succulents and ferns, can help to bring in a natural and organic feel. Consider adding a **floor plant** or a **hanging plant** to your room to add some visual interest and freshness.

6. Create a Focal Point

A focal point is an essential element of any well-styled room. A **focal point**, such as a statement piece of art or a colorful rug, can help to draw the eye and create a sense of visual interest. Consider creating a focal point in your room with a **bold-colored wall** or a **unique piece of furniture**.

7. Edit and Curate

Editing and curating are essential steps in the styling process. **Editing** involves removing any unnecessary or cluttered items from the room, while **curating** involves carefully selecting and arranging the remaining items to create a cohesive look. Consider **editing your bookshelf** or **curating your coffee table** to create a stylish and inviting space.

8. Add Metallic Accents

Metallic accents, such as gold, silver, and copper, can add a touch of glamour and sophistication to a room. **Metallic accents**, such as vases, picture frames, and lighting fixtures, can help to create a cohesive and stylish look. Consider adding a **gold vase** or a **copper lighting fixture** to your room to add some visual interest and elegance.

9. Incorporate Natural Elements

Natural elements, such as wood, stone, and brick, can add a touch of warmth and coziness to a room. **Incorporating natural elements**, such as a wooden headboard or a stone fireplace, can help to create a sense of organic and natural style. Consider adding a **wooden bench** or a **stone vase** to your room to bring in a natural and earthy feel.

10. Don't Be Afraid to Experiment

Experimenting with different styles and looks is an essential part of the styling process. **Trying out new things**, such as a bold-colored wall or a unique piece of furniture, can help to create a sense of excitement and freshness in a room. Consider **experimenting with different textures** or **trying out a new color palette** to create a stylish and inviting space.

11. Create a Cohesive Look

Creating a cohesive look is essential for any well-styled room. **Choosing a unifying theme**, such as a coastal or bohemian style, can help to create a sense of cohesion and harmony in a room. Consider **choosing a few core pieces**, such as a statement piece of art or a colorful rug, to create a cohesive and stylish look.

12. Add Personal Touches

Adding personal touches, such as family photos or heirlooms, can help to make a room feel truly personal and unique. **Incorporating personal items**, such as a vintage vase or a handmade quilt, can help to create a sense of warmth and coziness in a room. Consider **adding a few personal touches**, such as a favorite book or a sentimental object, to make your room feel truly special.

Some common mistakes to avoid when styling a room include **over-accessorizing**, **ignoring the 60-30-10 rule**, and **forgetting to add texture and pattern**. By avoiding these common mistakes and incorporating the styling secrets outlined above, you can create a room that is truly stylish and inviting.

Real-World Examples

A great example of a well-styled room is a living room that incorporates a neutral base, adds texture and pattern, and creates a focal point. For example, a living room with a neutral beige sofa can be paired with a patterned rug and a statement piece of art to create a cohesive and stylish look. Another example is a bedroom that incorporates natural elements, such as a wooden headboard and a stone vase, to create a sense of organic and natural style.
