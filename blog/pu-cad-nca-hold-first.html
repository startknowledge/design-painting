Introduction to PU CAD and NCA

PU CAD refers to the use of polyurethane coatings in computer-aided design (CAD) applications, while NCA stands for non-conductive adhesive. Both technologies have revolutionized the design and painting industry, offering unparalleled benefits in terms of efficiency, durability, and aesthetics. By combining PU CAD and NCA, professionals can create innovative designs that not only look stunning but also provide long-lasting protection against environmental factors.
