Introduction to David Hockney's Life and Work

David Hockney was born on July 9, 1937, in Bradford, England. He began his artistic journey at a young age, studying at the Bradford College of Art and later at the Royal College of Art in London. Hockney's early work was characterized by his bold and colorful depictions of Los Angeles swimming pools, which became a hallmark of his style. Throughout his career, he explored a wide range of mediums, including painting, drawing, printmaking, and photography.
